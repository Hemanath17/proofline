# Lessons

- Check facts in the packet before stating them (Daniel is the reviewer and Helen the partner, not the other way round)
- Call something an error only after a check or independent evidence confirms it; until then it is a lead
- Do not change an agreed stack or design without asking first
- Printed amounts may omit cents; parse numbers before comparing text to values
- Never use the line key as a claim's identity
- Do not add a library unless a stated need fails without it
- Do not bring up deadlines or time in recommendations unless asked
